## **Getting Started**

A simple guide to using Powfi: connect your Alephium wallet, get some ALPH, make your first swap, provide liquidity, or stake ALPH. Official application: [powfi.alephium.org](https://powfi.alephium.org/).

## **What you need**

To use Powfi, you need:

1. An [**Alephium wallet**](https://alephium.org/wallets/) to hold your assets and interact with DeFi.

2. Some **ALPH** for transactions and for swapping, providing liquidity, or staking.

3. **Powfi** for accessing its DEX and liquidity features.

## **What you can do on Powfi**

- **Swap tokens** through Powfi or directly through supported wallets, including the Alephium Mobile Wallet.

- **Stake ALPH** and receive **xALPH**. Staking is available through Powfi and supported wallets, including the Alephium Mobile Wallet.

- **Provide liquidity** through CLMM or CPMM pools on Powfi.

- **Farm** eligible liquidity positions.

- Use xALPH in supported pools and applications, including Powfi's ALPH/xALPH pool.

- Access liquidity connected to other ecosystems through **ETH and BSC**.

## **Your first steps**

**New to Powfi?** Start by getting some ALPH and connecting your Alephium wallet.

From there, you can:

**Swap →** trade tokens through Powfi or a supported wallet  
**Stake →** stake ALPH and receive xALPH through Powfi or a supported wallet  
**Provide liquidity →** choose a CLMM or CPMM pool on Powfi

## **Stay safe**

- **Never share your seed phrase or private key.**

- Make sure you are using [powfi.alephium.org](https://powfi.alephium.org/) or an official supported wallet.

- Check token and pool details before confirming a transaction.

- Only sign transactions you understand.
