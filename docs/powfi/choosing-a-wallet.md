Alephium offers several **non-custodial wallet options** for managing ALPH and using DeFi. This includes Alephium's own Mobile, Desktop and Extension wallets, as well as third-party software and hardware wallets.

## **Alephium Wallets**

Alephium provides its own **Mobile, Desktop and Browser Extension wallets**.

### Mobile Wallet

The **Alephium Mobile Wallet** is available on iOS and Android and is designed for everyday use.

![wallet-mobile-app](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5d43636-67f5-455e-b1bc-0d30395abe77)

- Manage ALPH on the go

- Swap tokens

- Stake ALPH and receive xALPH

- Connect to dApps such as Powfi with WalletConnect

- Buy ALPH with Visa/Mastercard, bank transfers, or Apple/Google Pay depending on your location.

- QR code and biometric security support

Download from:

- [Android Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.alephium.wallet)

- [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/alephium-wallet/id6469043072)

### Desktop Wallet

## **Hot / Cold Wallet Pattern**

A common security practice is to use different wallets for different purposes:

- A **hot wallet** is a software wallet connected to the internet, including **mobile, desktop and browser extension wallets**. It is useful for everyday activities like swapping, staking and interacting with DeFi applications. Keep only the amount you need for these activities.

- A **cold wallet** uses a hardware device to keep your private keys offline. It is suited to holding larger amounts that you do not need to access regularly.

Keeping your everyday DeFi funds in a hot wallet and your longer-term holdings in a cold wallet can help limit your exposure when interacting with DeFi applications.

## **Stay Safe**

**Never share your seed phrase or private keys.** Be cautious of phishing attempts and always make sure you are using the official wallet or dApp before connecting your wallet or signing a transaction.

*Wallet options are not exhaustive. Always do your own research before choosing a wallet.*
